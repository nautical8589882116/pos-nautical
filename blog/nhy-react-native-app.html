Nautical POS · NHY App

The floor changed. Your printed menu did not have to keep up alone.

How the NHY React Native app rebuilds the catalogue, the table QR, the stock room and the ad from one owner screen.

You already know the scene. A laminated menu that still lists last monsoon’s special. A table that waits ten minutes to catch a server’s eye. A bar that 86s a bottle mid-shift. A shop window that should have a new photo — and never gets one because the owner is on the till.

Those are floor problems. The NHY app inside Nautical POS is the owner console built to close them for restaurant owners, cafe owners, bar managers and retail shop owners. Guests never download an app.

Stop retyping the printed menu

An AI menu builder reads a printed-menu photo and builds categories and items. Then you add colour, imagery, price, calories, veg/spicy/featured flags, and Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner availability. Retail shops can import a Shopify CSV instead.
