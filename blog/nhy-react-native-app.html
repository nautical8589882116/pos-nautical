Stop retyping the printed menu

An AI menu builder starts from the paper you already have. Import from photo reads a printed menu picture and builds the categories and items. You then set a category colour and image, a price, calories, veg, spicy and featured flags, and availability by Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner. A café can hide the lunch board at 11. A shop does not force food fields onto a catalogue — Shopify CSV import brings the retail list in instead.

On the owner console the same screen is labelled Menu Builder. Import (AI) sits next to Add Category and Add Item. Catalog profiles switch the active fields: Restaurant, Café, Bar & Pub, Bakery, Retail Shop, Boutique and more. One app, the right fields for the room you run.

The table should not wait for a wave

Contactless ordering in NHY is a per-table QR code. The guest scans with the phone camera and lands on a web menu. There is no app download. They pick a portion size — Quarter, Half or Full — apply a coupon, and see a live summary with subtotal, service charge, tax and discount before they submit. That last screen matters. People stop when the total appears as a surprise at the till.

Restaurant Table 12 scans, chooses Full, and the ticket exists before anyone walks the floor.
Café A two-top orders while the barista is on the steam wand. The QR code ordering system does not need a spare pair of hands.
Bar A booth reorders a Half pour without fighting the rail for eye contact.
Retail shop A counter or fitting-room QR turns the catalogue into a ticket the same way a table does.

A ticket has a life. Watch it.

Once the order lands, it moves Pending, then Preparing, then Ready, then Completed. You can send the receipt from that same board. Filters cover date, table, payment method and status, so a bar manager can pull unpaid tabs and a shop owner can pull today’s card sales without a spreadsheet first. Cancelled sits beside the live states, so a void is a status, not a story someone has to remember.

The owner console calls this order history: daily, weekly, monthly or a custom range, plus table number, paid or unpaid, and that status list. The kitchen view is one tap away.

Find out the shelf is empty before the guest does

Inventory in NHY covers food stock and retail stock in the same place. Low-stock alerts fire before the 86. Purchase orders record what you asked the supplier for. Transfers move quantity between locations when one café is heavy and the other is light, or when a shop moves a size from the storeroom to the floor.

That is the retail inventory management app job and the kitchen job at once. A bar counts bottles. A boutique counts sizes. The floor should hear it before the guest does.

The photo that never got taken

The catalogue item becomes the campaign. A café special, a bar serve, a boutique piece — same studio, different product.

It covers the Tuesday when nobody had a free hand and the window still needed a picture.
