Stop retyping the menu. The floor is already moving.

How restaurant POS software, contactless ordering, and a retail inventory management app finally share one shift.

A printed menu sits on the counter. Lunch prices changed last Tuesday. Across the room a table has been waiting ten minutes to catch a server’s eye. In the storeroom, the last pack of a hero SKU is already gone. The marketing photo for this week’s mocktail was never taken.

That is one afternoon, not four different businesses. Restaurant owners, café owners, bar managers, and retail shop owners all lose the same minutes: catalog work that should have been a photo, guests who should have ordered themselves, stock that should have spoken up, and a campaign that never left the phone roll. Nautical POS is restaurant POS software built as an operating system for all four floors. Tap Tap Go. ⚓ Order in 3 Seconds.

Photograph the menu. Stop typing it.

The first job of any AI menu builder is not “look clever.” It is to get tonight’s list onto the floor without a second data-entry shift. In Nautical you photograph a printed menu. The import from photo (AI) reads the page and builds categories and items — names, structure, the skeleton you would otherwise retype.

From there you finish what a camera cannot guess. Categories get colour and imagery. Items get price, calories, veg / spicy / featured flags, and availability by Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner. Retail shops skip the photograph and bring a catalogue in with a Shopify CSV import. Same builder. Different door.

A QR code ordering system that does not ask for an app

Contactless ordering only works if the guest never has to hunt for software. Nautical prints a per-table QR code. The guest opens the phone camera, lands on a web menu, and orders. No download.

They pick a portion size — Quarter, Half, or Full — apply a coupon, and read a live summary before they submit: subtotal, service charge, tax, and discount. The bill is not a surprise at the end of the course. For a bar, that is a highball and a shareable plate without waving. For a café, it is a table that orders the second round while staff plates the first. For a restaurant, it is a full table that does not stall the pass. Retail can use the same contactless path when a shop floor needs a scan-and-pay lane that is not a second till.

The guest should see the tax before they tap submit. The kitchen should see the ticket before anyone walks it across the room.

The ticket has a path: Pending to Completed

An order that disappears into a notepad is how a bar loses a garnish and a shop loses a hold. Nautical’s pipeline is short on purpose: Pending → Preparing → Ready → Completed. Receipts go out from the same trail. Filters cut the list by date, table, payment method, and status so a manager is not scrolling the whole Saturday to find one UPI ticket from table 9.

That pipeline is the same feature on four floors. The restaurant uses table and course timing. The café uses it to keep the espresso window honest. The bar uses it so a ready drink does not sit while the ticket is still “somewhere.” The shop uses status and payment method to close a floor sale without a second notebook.

Stock that speaks before the mid-shift surprise

Discovering you are out mid-shift is not an inventory problem. It is a guest problem that arrived too late. Nautical inventory tracks Food stock and Retail stock in the same product. Low-stock alerts fire before the last unit is promised. Purchase orders cover the refill. Transfers move units between locations when one store is dry and the other is heavy.

A restaurant watches protein and garnish. A café watches beans and pastry. A bar watches bottles and citrus. A retail shop watches size runs and shelf packs. The labels change. The alert, the purchase order, and the transfer do not.

The photo that never got taken still has to ship

AI Marketing Studio is for the week you do not have a photographer. AI Trial Lab builds product mockups so a new pastry, a new pour, or a new shelf item can exist as an image before the shoot happens. AI-generated video ads can be cut for any item. AI try-on is for products guests need to see on a body or in a room — apparel, accessories, home — not only for a plated dish.

Marketing is not a fifth product. It sits on the same catalogue you already priced and flagged. If the item is live for Lunch, the ad can talk about Lunch.
